The method · DeepSCR

Four powers, separated — so claims get checked, not trusted.

Every engagement runs the loop. Nothing is "done" until its evidence trail resolves on disk. See the protocols →

Explore the four roles and four specialists
01 · Hypothesis

Scope the real thing

Recon the actual codebase, then a 6-question decomposition — no vague "use AI".

02 · Contradiction

Attack it first

Surface ≥3 failure modes before shipping. Doubt the method before, not after.

03 · Verification

Prove on ground truth

Inspect the referenced files and the results of the checks enabled for this engagement.

04 · Certification

Score 0–100

An FDE Assurance Score, written to a public, hash-chained registry anyone can verify.

Running it fully autonomous? The loop can be governed by the Frozen Arbiters: a sealed, tamper-evident contract and mutation-tested oracles with a measured baseline — arbiters the agent can plead to, but never edit. Read §6 of the protocols →
